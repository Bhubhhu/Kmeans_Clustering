Introduction & Problem

Immunisation inequity across 194 countries

0

Zero-dose infants

Still unreached despite high global averages; dropout between DTP1 and DTP3 persists.

Analytical gap

Grouping by geography or GDP hides similarities in supply chains, multi-dose retention and newer-vaccine rollout (PCV3, ROTA).

Question

How can we group countries on 11 WHO/GAVI indicators without arbitrary thresholds, validate stability, and avoid over-generalising?

Limitations & Responsible Use

Clusters are exploratory summaries, not permanent labels

Dynamic categories

Countries move between tiers as GAVI co-financing and cold-chain investment mature.

Ecological fallacy

National averages mask rural/urban gaps and conflict-affected zero-dose pockets.

Future scope

Sub-national DHS/MICS data · time-series clustering 2000–2025 · Gaussian Mixture Models.
